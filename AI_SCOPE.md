# AI Roadmap

Predex is designed with an extensible architecture that allows Artificial Intelligence to be integrated as the platform grows.

Rather than replacing the exchange, AI acts as an intelligence layer that helps users make informed decisions, improves market quality, and enhances platform security.

The goal is to build an AI-powered prediction market where users can not only trade opinions but also understand why markets move.

---

## AI Market Insights

Generate AI-powered summaries for every market by analyzing historical data, news, trends, and other relevant information.

Example:

> India has won 8 of the last 10 matches against Australia. The market currently prices India's winning probability at 64%.

---

## AI Research Assistant

An AI assistant capable of answering market-related questions.

Examples:

- Why is this market price increasing?
- What factors are affecting this prediction?
- Explain today's trading activity.
- Summarize the latest news impacting this market.

---

## AI Probability Engine

Develop machine learning models that estimate the probability of an event based on historical data and external signals.

The platform can compare:

- AI Estimated Probability
- Current Market Probability

This helps users identify potentially underpriced or overpriced markets.

---

## AI Trading Assistant

An intelligent assistant that analyzes market conditions and provides data-driven insights.

Example:

- Current Market Price
- Estimated Fair Value
- Risk Level
- Supporting Factors

The assistant provides analysis rather than financial advice.

---

## AI Market Creation

Use AI to automatically generate prediction markets from structured event data.

Examples:

- Sports fixtures
- Elections
- Financial events
- Technology announcements

The AI can also generate market descriptions and settlement rules.

---

## AI Market Sentiment

Analyze public sentiment from news articles, social media, and other public sources.

Display metrics such as:

- Positive Sentiment
- Negative Sentiment
- Trending Topics
- Market Confidence

---

## AI Recommendation System

Recommend markets based on:

- User interests
- Trading history
- Watchlists
- Market activity

---

## AI Fraud Detection

Use machine learning to identify suspicious activities such as:

- Wash trading
- Market manipulation
- Bot activity
- Unusual trading behavior
- Abnormal order patterns

---

## AI Risk Engine

Generate risk scores for users and markets based on trading patterns and liquidity.

This helps improve platform stability and security.

---

## AI Copilot

A conversational AI assistant that understands the platform.

Examples:

> Find undervalued markets.

> Explain today's biggest price movement.

> Show high-volume markets.

> Compare AI predictions with market prices.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Predex aims to evolve from a prediction exchange into an **AI-powered prediction intelligence platform**, combining modern exchange infrastructure with artificial intelligence to deliver smarter insights, better market transparency, and more informed decision-making.

The vision is to build a platform where users don't just trade opinions—they understand the information and reasoning that drive those opinions.
